What counts as a failed delivery?

Any final DLR with a status other than DELIVRD, plus messages with no DLR after 72 hours.

How is TPS measured?

Submits accepted per second, averaged over a 10 second window on each bind.

Who gets paged?

The on-call engineer first, then the platform lead after 15 minutes without acknowledgement.

A single details element

Works without any class.

Prose

Why the night shift watches DLR time

Submit success tells you the operator accepted a message. It does not tell you the handset got it. The delivery report arrives later, and the gap between the two is where most real trouble hides.

When that gap stretches from a few seconds to a minute and a half, something upstream is queueing. The fix is rarely on our side, but knowing early lets the team shift traffic before customers notice. Inline code still uses JetBrains Mono inside prose.
